The package

What your course includes

The package combines a three-session PRP course with derma microneedling and infrared. Your clinician will explain each treatment and assess whether the course is appropriate for you.

Platelet-rich plasma

PRP stands for platelet-rich plasma. A small sample of your blood is processed to separate the platelet-rich plasma, which is then injected into the scalp.

Derma microneedling

Included in the course as a separate step alongside PRP. Your clinician will describe the device used and what this step involves at your assessment.

Infrared treatment

Included in the course. Your clinician will explain what this step involves and how it fits your plan at your assessment.

Quick answers

Questions about Hair PRP

What does the course include

The three-session course combines PRP, derma microneedling and infrared. See the package details above the enquiry form for pricing and confirmed inclusions.

What happens during PRP

A small blood sample is taken and processed to separate the platelet-rich plasma. The PRP is then injected into the scalp.

How is suitability assessed

A clinical assessment is needed before treatment can be recommended. Your clinician will review your medical history and discuss your hair concerns and expectations.

When can I assess the results

Response to treatment varies. At your assessment, ask when progress should be reviewed and whether further sessions may be recommended.

Does sending the form commit me to treatment

No. It asks our team to contact you about a consultation. You can discuss the course and decide whether to proceed after your assessment.
