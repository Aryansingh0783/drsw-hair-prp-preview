Questions

Five answers before you send the form.

What does the package include?

Three sessions combining PRP, derma microneedling and infrared. £950 is the total package price.

What happens during the PRP part?

The clinic takes a small blood sample and uses a centrifuge to separate the platelet-rich plasma. The PRP is then injected into the scalp.

When might I notice a change?

The clinic's PRP page says visible results may appear after one to three months. Results vary, and maintenance may be recommended.

Is there any downtime?

The clinic describes little or no recovery time for PRP alone. This package includes two additional steps, so ask the team about the full appointment and the aftercare that follows it.

Does sending the form commit me to anything?

No. It asks the clinic to contact you. Treatment is confirmed only after the clinic has reviewed your medical history and discussed your goals with you.
