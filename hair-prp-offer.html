07 · Questions

Five useful answers before you enquire.

What does the £950 package include?

It includes three sessions and combines PRP, derma microneedling and infrared. £950 is the total campaign package price. The clinic's published price for a three-session PRP course on its own is £1,500, taken as one session per month.

What happens during the PRP part?

The clinic takes a small blood sample and uses a centrifuge to separate the platelet-rich plasma. The PRP is then injected into the scalp.

When might I notice a change?

The clinic's PRP page says visible results may appear after 1 to 3 months. Results vary, and maintenance may be recommended.

Is there any downtime?

The clinic describes little or no recovery time for PRP alone. This package includes two additional steps, so ask the team about the full appointment and aftercare.

How does the clinic decide whether it is suitable?

The clinic reviews your medical history and discusses your treatment goals. It then confirms whether the package is appropriate and explains what to expect.
